Section 11 of 12: Letter of Informed Consent
You have met all eligibility criteria. Please read the informed consent document below carefully before proceeding.

This research study is being conducted by Bikram Patnaik who is a doctoral candidate at Westcliff University. This study is being performed in partial fulfillment of the doctoral degree requirements for the Doctor of Business Administration (DBA).

The title of this study is Harnessing Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare Leadership: Evaluating Strategic Decision-Making Effectiveness and Adoption Challenges.

Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this qualitative case study is to explore why U.S. healthcare decision-makers adopt or do not adopt Artificial Intelligence (AI) for strategic decision-making, and what factors shape that process. The study examines decision-makers' perceptions of AI usefulness, ease of use, and trustworthiness, the role of transformational leadership in AI adoption, organizational readiness, and the cognitive, ethical, and organizational challenges associated with AI implementation.

Participant Criteria

You are being invited to participate in this study because you meet the following eligibility criteria: you are 25 years of age or older, currently hold a role with direct involvement in organizational or strategic decision-making within a U.S.-based healthcare setting, have a minimum of 5 years of experience in healthcare decision-making, and have direct experience with or exposure to AI initiatives in your organization. Individuals are excluded from participation if they are employed by an AI vendor or consulting firm, have participated in a similar study within the past 12 months, are unable to communicate fluently in English, or have been in their current role for less than six months.

Participant Tasks and Time Commitment

If you agree to participate in this study, you will be asked to participate in one semi-structured interview lasting approximately 45 to 60 minutes conducted via a secure virtual meeting platform such as Google Meet or Zoom. The interview will consist of open-ended questions about your experiences, perceptions, and challenges regarding AI adoption and strategic decision-making in your organization. The interview will be audio-recorded with your consent. Total time commitment is approximately 45 to 60 minutes.

Participant Rights

Your participation in this study is completely voluntary. You may withdraw your consent to participate at any time without penalty. If you withdraw your consent, you may request to have your data removed from the study. This study has been reviewed and certified by the Institutional Review Board (IRB) at Westcliff University. For problems or questions regarding participants' rights, you can contact the IRB chair by email at IRB@westcliff.edu.

Risks

This study poses no more than minimal risk to participants. There is a minimal risk that participants' professional information or interview responses could be disclosed. This risk will be mitigated through secure data storage, restricted access, and removal of identifying information from transcripts and reports. Participants may experience mild professional or emotional discomfort when discussing organizational challenges, leadership practices, ethical concerns, or difficulties associated with AI adoption. Participants are free to skip any question or withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.

Benefits

There are no direct or immediate personal benefits for participants in this research. The potential benefits of this research include contributing evidence-based insights that can inform leadership development strategies, organizational readiness initiatives, and AI governance frameworks for healthcare organizations seeking to adopt AI for strategic decision-making.

Compensation or Incentives

You will receive no compensation or incentives for your participation in this study.

Data Collection and Protection

Interview responses and demographic information will be collected as part of this study. Participants will be assigned unique alphanumeric codes (e.g., P1, P2, P3), and names and other direct identifiers will be removed from transcripts and research reports. Your name will not be used in any publication or presentation of the findings. Participants' employers or organizations will not be provided with individual interview responses. The participant identification key linking names to codes will be stored in a separate, encrypted file accessible only to the researcher.

Data Security

Human subjects research regulations require that data be kept for a minimum of three (3) years. All research materials, consent forms, interview recordings, transcripts, and data collected will be stored electronically in encrypted, password-protected files on a password-protected device accessible only to the researcher. The dissertation chair may review de-identified data as part of the dissertation supervision process. Only the principal investigator, faculty supervisors, and the Institutional Review Board will have access to the data, or as required by law. After the required retention period, all electronic files will be permanently deleted, and any physical documents will be shredded.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this study or concerns about your participation, you can contact the principal investigator Bikram Patnaik by phone (408) 493-9465 or email b.patnaik.192@westcliff.edu at any time. For problems or questions regarding participants' rights, you can contact the IRB chair by email at IRB@westcliff.edu.

Researchers alone are responsible for carrying out their approved plans.
